Why L̃₂≈0.98 at P=5000 with no outer wells
------------------------------------------
L̃₂ = λ₂/(λ₂+ρ') with ρ'=κ/(a0 P). Outer wells inflate λ₂ during the mid-P
ramp, but once λ₂∼4.5e-3 is set, shrinking ρ' alone drives L̃₂→1. At P=5000
the landscape reorganizes onto |μ|≈0.7 (π_out=0); λ₂ stays ∼same order so
learnability stays high. Lab teacher is still y=Uỹ≈(0.31,0.95), not pure He3.
