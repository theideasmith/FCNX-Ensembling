Eigenframe SADDLE action with diagonalized Q
============================================

Spec:
  1. Diagonalize lab Q → Λ (eigenframe)
  2. Teacher ỹ = (0, ε) in that frame
  3. Amplitudes G̃ from diagonal Λ only (no lab A = U G̃ Uᵀ)
  4. V(w) = prior + ½ Σ G̃_kk φ_k², φ = Uᵀ c(w)
  5. Saddle point: Q comes from the global minimizer of V only
     (hard assignment — not a Boltzmann/Laplace mixture over wells)

Params: d=10, N=χ=400, sa0=1, κ=0.5, ε=1

Results (* = occupied saddle):
  P=50    L3=0.258  saddle μ=0.54  (bulk only)
  P=200   L3=0.626  saddle μ=3.66  (outer; bulk still a local min)
  P=1000  L3=0.901  saddle μ=4.33
  P=5000  L3=0.979  saddle μ=4.50

Plot: V_vs_w_saddle_d10.png
Run:  julia eigenframe_diag_q_action.jl --d 10 --n1 400 --chi 400 --sa0 1 --kappa 0.5 --eps 1
