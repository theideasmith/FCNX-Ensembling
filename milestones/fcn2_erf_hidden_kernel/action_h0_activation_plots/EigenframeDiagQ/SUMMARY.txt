Eigenframe action with diagonalized Q (user spec)
================================================

Equations (eigenframe only — no lab A = U G̃ Uᵀ):
  Q = U Λ Uᵀ
  ỹ = (0, ε)          # cubic eigenchannel
  G̃ = diag from Λ,ỹ  # near-null |λ| < 0.01 max|λ| → G̃_kk = 0
  V(w) = (d/2s0) w² + ½ Σ_k G̃_kk φ_k(w)²,  φ = Uᵀ c(w)

Q rebuilt each iteration from wells of V
  (default: Laplace mixture; --hard: global-min well only).

Params: d=10, N=χ=400, sa0=1, κ=0.5, ε=1

Mixture L3(P):
  P=50    L3=0.235   wells μ≈0.48, 2.28
  P=200   L3=0.626   wells μ≈0.57, 3.66   (outer is deeper)
  P=1000  L3=0.901   wells μ≈0.58, 4.33
  P=5000  L3=0.979   wells μ≈0.58, 4.50

Hard-saddle L3(P) matches at P≥200 (outer is the Hamiltonian min).
