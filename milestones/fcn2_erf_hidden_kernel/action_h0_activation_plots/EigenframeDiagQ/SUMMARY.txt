Eigenframe + Laplace mixture saddle weights
===========================================

Basis (only change vs usual multiwell Laplace):
  Q = U Λ Uᵀ
  ỹ = (0, ε)
  G̃ from diagonal Λ (no lab A = U G̃ Uᵀ)
  V(w) = prior + ½ Σ G̃_kk φ_k²,  φ = Uᵀ c(w)

Mixture saddle weights:
  π_k ∝ m_k σ_k exp(−V_k)   (m=2 for ±μ)
  Q = Σ π_k Q(μ_k, σ_k)

Action plotted on w ∈ [−wmax, wmax] so both ± wells are visible.

Params: d=10, N=χ=400, sa0=1, κ=0.5, ε=1

  P=1000  L3=0.901  wells ±0.58, ±4.33 (π_out≈1)
